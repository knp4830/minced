- 2 | cup | napa cabbage | finely chopped
- 1 | tsp | salt |
- 8 | oz | ground pork |
- 2 | | scallion | finely chopped
- 2 | clove | garlic | grated
- 1 | tsp | ginger | grated
- 1 | tbsp | soy sauce |
- 1 | tbsp | sake |
- 1 | tsp | toasted sesame oil |
- 0.5 | tsp | white pepper |

- 0.5 | cup | water |
- 1 | tbsp | soy sauce | for dipping

1. Toss the cabbage with salt, rest 10 min, then squeeze out all the liquid.
2. Mix the cabbage with the pork, scallion, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sake, sesame oil and pepper until sticky.
3. Place 1 tsp filling in each wrapper. Wet the edge, fold in half, and pleat the front edge 5-6 times, pressing shut.

7. Mix the dipping soy sauce and vinegar. Serve hot.

- 2 | tbsp | soy sauce |
- 1 | tbsp | sake |
- 1 | tbsp | mirin |

- 1 | tsp | ginger | grated
- 0.5 | cup | potato starch |

1. Toss the chicken with the soy sauce, sake, mirin, garlic and ginger. Marinate at least 30 min.
2. Drain off any extra liquid. Coat each piece in potato starch, pressing it on.
3. Heat oil to 320 F (160 C). Fry in batches 3-4 min, until pale gold. Rest on a rack 4 min.
