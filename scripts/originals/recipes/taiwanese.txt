- 1 | cup | shallot | thinly sliced

- 4 | clove | garlic | minced

- 0.25 | cup | soy sauce |
- 2 | tbsp | dark soy sauce |

- 6 | | egg | hard-boiled, peeled
- 6 | cup | white rice | cooked, for serving
1. Fry the shallots in the oil in a pot over medium heat 8-10 min, until deep golden. Lift out half for topping.
2. Add the pork and cook 8 min, until browned. Add the garlic for 30 sec.

5. Spoon over rice with an egg and the reserved fried shallots.

2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat. Fry ginger, garlic and scallion 2 min. Add doubanjiang and fry 2 min, until the oil turns red.

@ san-bei-ji | San Bei Ji (Three Cup Chicken) | Taiwanese | 15 | 25 | 4 | 1 | Skillet | Throw the basil in at the very end and cover for a few seconds so it perfumes the steam.
- 1.5 | lb | chicken thighs | boneless, chopped into 2-inch pieces
- 2 | tbsp | toasted sesame oil |
- 1 | piece | ginger | 2 inches, sliced
- 10 | clove | garlic | whole, peeled
- 3 | | dried red chiles |
- 0.25 | cup | soy sauce |
- 0.25 | cup | shaoxing wine |
- 1 | tbsp | rock sugar |
- 1 | cup | thai basil | leaves
1. Heat the sesame oil in a heavy skillet or clay pot over medium heat. Fry ginger, garlic and chiles 2 min, until golden at the edges.
2. Add the chicken and cook 5 min, turning, until lightly browned.
3. Add the soy sauce, wine and sugar. Cover and simmer on low 15 min, until the chicken is cooked through.
4. Uncover and raise the heat. Cook 3-4 min, stirring, until the sauce reduces to a sticky glaze.
5. Off the heat, fold in the basil, cover 20 sec, and serve with rice.

@ cong-you-bing | Cong You Bing (Scallion Pancakes) | Taiwanese | 40 | 15 | 4 | 0 | Skillet | Hot water in the dough keeps the pancakes tender and layered, not chewy.
- 2 | cup | all-purpose flour |
- 0.75 | cup | water | steaming hot
- 1 | tsp | salt | divided
- 3 | tbsp | toasted sesame oil | for brushing
- 6 | | scallion | thinly sliced
- 3 | tbsp | vegetable oil | for frying
- 2 | tbsp | soy sauce |
- 1 | tbsp | rice vinegar |
- 1 | tsp | granulated sugar |
1. Stir the flour and 1/2 tsp salt in a bowl and add the hot water. Mix to a shaggy dough, then knead 5 min, until smooth. Cover and rest 30 min.
2. Divide into 4 pieces. Roll each into a very thin round.
3. Brush with sesame oil, sprinkle with salt and scallion, then roll into a tight log and coil into a snail. Press flat and roll to a 6-inch round.
4. Fry each in a skillet in a little oil over medium heat 3 min per side, until deep golden and flaky.
5. Whisk the soy sauce, vinegar and sugar. Cut the pancakes in wedges and serve with the sauce.

@ gua-bao | Gua Bao (Pork Belly Buns) | Taiwanese | 40 | 90 | 6 | 0 | Pot,Skillet | Brush the folded buns with oil before steaming to keep them from sticking together.
- 12 | oz | pork belly | skin-on or off, in a slab
- 0.25 | cup | soy sauce |
- 2 | tbsp | shaoxing wine |
- 2 | tbsp | rock sugar |
- 2 | | star anise |
- 1 | | cinnamon stick |
- 2 | cup | water |
- 2 | cup | all-purpose flour | for the buns
- 1 | tsp | active dry yeast |
- 1 | tbsp | granulated sugar |
- 0.5 | cup | water | warm, for the dough
- 1 | tbsp | vegetable oil |
- 0.5 | cup | chinese pickled mustard | chopped
- 0.5 | cup | fresh cilantro | chopped
- 0.25 | cup | peanuts | roasted, crushed with 1 tbsp sugar
1. Blanch the pork belly in boiling water for 5 min, then drain.
2. Simmer the pork with the soy sauce, wine, rock sugar, star anise, cinnamon and 2 cups water, covered, on low 75 min, until very tender. Slice into thick slabs.
3. Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, oil and warm water. Knead 8 min until smooth. Cover and rise 1 hour until doubled.
4. Divide into 6 pieces; roll each to an oval, brush with oil, and fold over. Rest 20 min.
5. Steam the buns over boiling water 12 min, until puffy and set.
6. Open each bun and fill with pork slab, pickled mustard, cilantro and the peanut-sugar.

@ taiwanese-popcorn-chicken | Taiwanese Popcorn Chicken (Yan Su Ji) | Taiwanese | 20 | 15 | 4 | 1 | Dutch oven | Fry the basil last; it turns into crisp green flakes in seconds.
- 1.5 | lb | chicken thighs | boneless, skinless, 1-inch pieces
- 1 | tbsp | soy sauce |
- 1 | tbsp | shaoxing wine |
- 3 | clove | garlic | grated
- 1 | tsp | ginger | grated
- 0.5 | tsp | chinese five spice |
- 1 | tsp | granulated sugar |
- 1 | | egg |
- 0.75 | cup | potato starch | sweet potato starch is traditional and gives a craggier crust
- 0.25 | cup | vegetable oil | about 3 cups to fill the pot; roughly this much is absorbed
- 1 | cup | thai basil | leaves, dry
- 1 | tsp | salt |
- 0.5 | tsp | white pepper |
1. Toss the chicken with the soy sauce, wine, garlic, ginger, five-spice, sugar and egg. Marinate 30 min.
2. Dredge each piece in sweet potato starch, pressing it on.
3. Heat about 3 cups oil in a pot to 350 F (175 C). Fry the chicken in batches 3-4 min, until deep golden. Drain.
4. Raise the oil to 375 F and re-fry for 1 min until crisp.
5. Fry the basil 10-15 sec until crisp. Drain.
6. Toss the chicken with the basil, salt and white pepper and serve hot.
