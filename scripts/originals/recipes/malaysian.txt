1. Rub the chicken all over with 1 tbsp salt. Place in a pot with half the ginger, 2 scallions and enough water to cover. Bring to a boil, turn off the heat, cover and rest 40 min until a thermometer in the thigh reads 165 F (74 C).
2. Plunge the chicken into ice water 10 min. Rub with sesame oil and soy sauce. Keep the stock.
3. Fry 3 minced garlic cloves and 1 tbsp minced ginger in the rendered chicken fat 1 min. Add the rice and stir 1 min.

6. Chop the chicken. Serve over rice with cucumber, chili sauce and a bowl of the warm stock.

@ roti-canai | Roti Canai | Malaysian | 40 | 15 | 4 | 0 | Griddle | Rest the dough balls in oil for hours; that is what lets them stretch paper-thin.
- 3 | cup | all-purpose flour |
- 1 | tsp | salt |
- 1 | tbsp | granulated sugar |
- 1 | | egg |
- 0.75 | cup | water | warm
- 0.25 | cup | evaporated milk |
- 3 | tbsp | ghee | melted, plus more for cooking
1. Mix flour, salt and sugar. Add the egg, water, milk and 1 tbsp ghee. Knead 8-10 min, until smooth and elastic.
2. Divide into 6 balls. Coat each with melted ghee, cover and rest at least 1 hour, or up to overnight.
3. Oil your work surface. Flatten a ball, then stretch it as thin as paper with your hands, until you can see the surface through it.
4. Fold the edges to the center to make a square, or coil it into a spiral and flatten lightly.
5. Cook on a hot griddle with a little ghee, 2 min per side, until golden and crisp at the edges.
6. Slap the hot roti between your hands to fluff and separate the layers. Serve with dal or curry.
