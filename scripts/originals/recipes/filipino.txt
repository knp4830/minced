- 1 | | yellow onion | quartered
- 1 | tbsp | achiote paste | dissolved in 1/4 cup hot water

@ sisig | Sisig (Sizzling Pork) | Filipino | 20 | 50 | 4 | 3 | Skillet | Boil, grill, then chop; the crisp bits are what separate sisig from hash.

- 4 | oz | chicken liver |
- 1 | | yellow onion | finely diced, divided
- 3 | | bird's eye chili | minced
- 3 | tbsp | calamansi | juice
- 2 | tbsp | soy sauce |
- 0.5 | tsp | black pepper |
- 1 | tbsp | vegetable oil |
- 1 | | egg | one per serving, to crack on top
- 6 | cup | water | for boiling
- 1 | tsp | salt |
1. Boil the pork belly in the salted water 30 min, until tender. Drain and pat very dry.
2. Grill or broil the pork and liver until the pork skin blisters and the liver is cooked through, 5 min per side for the pork, 3 min per side for the liver.
3. Chop the pork and liver into fine pieces, no larger than 1/4 inch.

@ leche-flan | Leche Flan | Filipino | 20 | 60 | 8 | 0 | Baking dish | Strain the custard twice for a silky, bubble-free finish.
- 0.5 | cup | granulated sugar | for the caramel
- 10 | | egg yolk |
- 14 | oz | sweetened condensed milk | one can
- 1 | can | evaporated milk | 12 oz
- 1 | tsp | vanilla extract |
- 1 | tsp | lemon | zest
1. Melt the sugar in a small pan over medium heat, without stirring, 5 min until amber. Pour into the base of a 7-inch round dish and tilt to coat.
2. Whisk the yolks gently with the condensed milk, evaporated milk, vanilla and zest, stirring slowly so as not to make foam.
3. Strain the custard through a fine sieve into the dish.
4. Cover tightly with foil. Set the dish in a larger pan of hot water that reaches halfway up the sides.
5. Bake at 325 F (160 C) for 50-60 min, until the center barely jiggles and a knife comes out clean.
6. Cool, then chill at least 4 hours. Run a knife around the edge and invert onto a plate.
