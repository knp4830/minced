@ char-siu | Char Siu (Cantonese Barbecue Pork) | Chinese | 20 | 50 | 6 | 0 | Sheet pan | Marinate overnight; the glaze only sticks and chars properly on well-seasoned meat.
- 2 | lb | pork shoulder | cut in long 2-inch strips
- 3 | tbsp | hoisin sauce |
- 2 | tbsp | soy sauce |
- 2 | tbsp | honey | divided
- 1 | tbsp | shaoxing wine |
- 2 | tbsp | granulated sugar |
- 1 | tsp | chinese five spice |
- 1 | tbsp | fermented bean curd | optional, for color and depth
- 3 | clove | garlic | minced
1. Mix the hoisin, soy sauce, 1 tbsp honey, wine, sugar, five-spice, bean curd and garlic. Coat the pork well, cover and chill 8 hours or overnight.
2. Heat the oven to 425 F (220 C). Set the pork on a rack over a foil-lined sheet pan with a little water in the pan.
3. Roast 25 min. Brush with leftover marinade, flip and roast 15 min more.
4. Brush with the remaining honey and broil 2-4 min, watching closely, until the edges char in spots.
5. Rest 10 min, then slice across the grain. Serve with rice and greens.
