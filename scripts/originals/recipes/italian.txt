@ spaghetti-carbonara | Spaghetti alla Carbonara | Italian | 10 | 15 | 4 | 0 | Pot,Skillet | Mix the eggs and cheese off the heat; the pan's warmth alone cooks them to a cream.
- 12 | oz | spaghetti |
- 5 | oz | guanciale | cut in short strips
- 4 | | egg yolk |
- 2 | | egg |
- 3.5 | oz | pecorino romano | finely grated (about 1 cup), plus more to serve
- 1 | tsp | black pepper | freshly cracked
1. Whisk the yolks, whole eggs, pecorino and most of the pepper in a bowl to a thick paste.
2. Cook the guanciale in a cold skillet over medium-low heat 8-10 min, until the fat renders and the pieces are crisp. Turn off the heat.
3. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 1 cup of the pasta water, then drain.

5. Pour in the egg mixture and toss fast, adding pasta water a splash at a time, until the sauce is creamy and clings to the strands.
6. Serve at once with more pecorino and pepper.

@ risotto-alla-milanese | Risotto alla Milanese (Saffron Risotto) | Italian | 10 | 30 | 4 | 0 | Pot | Add the broth a ladle at a time and keep stirring; that is how the rice releases its starch.
- 5 | cup | chicken broth | kept hot
-  |  | saffron | a generous pinch of threads
- 3 | tbsp | unsalted butter | divided
- 1 | | shallot | finely chopped
- 1.5 | cup | carnaroli rice |
- 0.5 | cup | dry white wine |
- 0.75 | cup | parmesan | finely grated
- 0.5 | tsp | salt | or to taste
1. Steep the saffron in 1/2 cup of the hot broth for 10 min.
2. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a heavy pot over medium heat. Cook the shallot 3 min until soft.
3. Add the rice and stir 2 min, until the grains turn translucent at the edges. Pour in the wine and cook until absorbed.
4. Add hot broth a ladle at a time, stirring, letting each addition absorb before the next. After 12 min, stir in the saffron liquid.

6. Off the heat, beat in the remaining butter and the parmesan. Rest 2 min and serve; the texture should flow like lava.

- 3 | tbsp | olive oil |
- 4 | oz | pancetta | finely diced
- 1 | | yellow onion | finely diced
- 1 | | carrot | finely diced
- 1 | stalk | celery | finely diced
- 8 | oz | ground beef |
- 8 | oz | ground pork |
- 0.5 | cup | dry white wine |
- 0.5 | cup | whole milk |
- 2 | tbsp | tomato paste |
- 1 | cup | beef broth |
- 1 | tsp | salt |
- 1 | lb | fettuccine | or fresh tagliatelle
- 0.5 | cup | parmesan | grated
1. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat. Cook the pancetta 4 min, then the onion, carrot and celery 10 min, until very soft and sweet.
2. Add the beef and pork, break up with a spoon, and cook 10 min, until the juices cook off and the meat just begins to brown.
3. Pour in the wine and simmer until evaporated. Add the milk and cook until absorbed.

6. Cook the pasta in well-salted water until al dente. Toss with the ragù and a spoon of pasta water.
7. Serve with parmesan.
