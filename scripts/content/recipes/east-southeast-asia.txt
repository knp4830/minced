@ kimchi-jjigae | Kimchi Jjigae (Kimchi Stew) | Korean | 10 | 30 | 4 | 3 | Pot | Sour, well-fermented kimchi makes the best stew. Fresh kimchi tastes flat.
- 2 | cup | kimchi | aged, chopped, plus 1/4 cup of its brine
- 8 | oz | pork belly | sliced 1/4 inch thick
- 1 | tbsp | toasted sesame oil |
- 1 | tbsp | gochugaru |
- 1 | tbsp | gochujang |
- 3 | clove | garlic | minced
- 1 | tsp | granulated sugar |
- 1 | tbsp | soy sauce |
- 3.5 | cup | water |
- 14 | oz | firm tofu | cut in 1-inch cubes
- 0.5 | | yellow onion | sliced
- 2 | | scallion | sliced
1. Heat the sesame oil in a pot over medium-high heat. Brown the pork belly 4-5 min until the edges color.
2. Add kimchi, garlic, gochugaru, and sugar. Stir-fry 3 min until the kimchi turns glossy and slightly translucent.
3. Stir in gochujang and soy sauce, then pour in the water and kimchi brine. Bring to a boil.
4. Reduce to a steady simmer and cook 15-18 min, until the pork is tender and the broth is sour and savory.
5. Add tofu and onion. Simmer 5 min until the tofu is hot through.
6. Top with scallion and serve with rice.
