# UI and accessibility audit, 2026-11-04

Report only. No app code was changed. Base: `main` at d2b7c52. Dev server on port 3123, own browser tab, viewports 1440x900 and 375x812.

**Method.** A scripted pass on every page: horizontal overflow, heading order, landmarks, tap targets under 44 px, missing alt, unlabeled controls, text contrast (computed from real foreground and background colours, walking up ancestors), text under 12 px, content left/right margins, and a focus-ring test. Token ratios were computed by hand (WCAG relative luminance). Not measured: text over photographs (hero, card overlays), screen-reader behaviour, 200% zoom, the sign-in-only screens.

**Honest limits.**
- Contrast of text over photos cannot be measured by the script. Check the hero and photo-credit overlays by eye or with a sampled-pixel tool.
- Card titles on `/recipes` and the landing strip are links whose click area is the whole card (`after:absolute`). The script reports them as 26 px tall; the real hit area is the card, so they are not counted below.
- The programmatic focus test could not tell "no ring" from "ring only on keyboard focus", so it was replaced by a check on `:focus-visible` after a real Tab keypress. Result on the landing page: 35 of 35 tab stops show a ring (the hero search shows its ring on the wrapper through `focus-within`).

## Summary

32 findings: **1 high, 6 medium, 25 low.** Zero text-contrast failures on any of the 17 pages checked (outside photos). Zero images without `alt`, zero unlabeled inputs, zero nameless buttons or links, no horizontal scroll at 375 on any page. The weak spots are target size, left-hugging layouts, one navigation gap on phones, and one very long tab sequence.

## Ranked top 10

1. **H1 (high).** The site header on phones shows only the basket and Log in. "Recipes" and the fridge link are hidden below 640 px (`siteHeader.tsx:29,36`), while the landing nav shows them. On a phone, a signed-in user on any inner page has no header route to the fridge, the product's differentiator.
2. **F1 and C1 (medium).** Centering pattern: signed-out `/fridge` and `/pantry` content starts at x=40 and leaves 760 px empty on the right at 1440; Settings uses `max-w-xl` inside `page-w` (`settings/page.tsx:40-41`) and does the same. The legal pages sit about 96 px off-centre (A2).
3. **R1 (medium).** Filter chips are 32 px tall (40 px on touch), under the project's 44 px rule (`ui/chip.tsx:19-20`). Thirteen or more are visible at once on `/recipes`, plus 32 px disclosure summaries (R5).
4. **A1 (medium).** `/about` renders every photo credit open: 1,120 links, so a keyboard user tabs through about 1,100 stops (`about/page.tsx:98` `<details open>`).
5. **L1 (medium).** Landing hero: eyebrow, headline, a 44-word paragraph (`copy.ts:12`), search card and a stats line. At 375 the search card starts about 460 px down and the caption sits on the fold.
6. **P1 (medium).** Serving steppers on the recipe page are 36x36 from the `sm` breakpoint up (`servingsScaler.tsx:98`, `sm:size-9`).
7. **R2 (low, visible).** Slider rail `--color-track` #d8dcd4 is 1.24:1 on the canvas, below the 3:1 non-text rule (`filterSlider.tsx:127`, `globals.css:25`). The thumb is dark herb; the rail nearly disappears.
8. **R6 (low).** Unselected chip outline (`--color-line-strong`, 16% ink) is about 1.2:1 on canvas. The text carries the meaning, but the control boundary is almost invisible.
9. **U1 and R4 (low).** Heading order: the auth pages put an `h2` marketing line before the `h1` (`(auth)/layout.tsx:22`); `/recipes` puts the `h2` "Filters" before the `h1`.
10. **L2 (low).** "Drafted with AI, reviewed by Minced" links on recipe cards are 19 px tall on desktop and 34 px on mobile (`recipeCard.tsx:185`).

## Notes for the design session

Items relevant to the owner's seven-point list, observed but not redesigned here:
- **Hero wordiness (1):** L1. The photo caption pill sits at the fold on phones.
- **Fridge layout (2, 5):** F1. The signed-out gate is a single left-aligned column.
- **Sliders and flat colour (3, 7):** R2, R6, R8. Rail, chip outline and photoless tiles carry very little contrast against a sage canvas; the surface tokens (`canvas`, `band`, `sunken`, `track`, `stripe`) sit within a few percent of each other.
- **Navbar emptiness (4):** H1, H2, L4.
- **Centering (6):** F1, C1, C2, A2 share one cause: a narrow inner column inside `page-w` without `mx-auto`.
